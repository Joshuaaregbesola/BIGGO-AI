# BIGGO AI 🚀
### Think Big. Create Bigger.

BIGGO AI is an intelligent AI platform designed to help people learn, solve problems, explore ideas, create content, and build projects.

## ✨ Features

- 🧠 **BIGGO Brain** – Ask questions and get intelligent answers.
- 📚 **BIGGO Study** – Learn subjects and understand difficult topics.
- 🎨 **BIGGO Create** – Write, brainstorm, and bring ideas to life.
- 🌍 **BIGGO Explore** – Discover information and research topics.
- 💻 **BIGGO Builder** – Get help building websites, apps, and projects.
- 👁️ **BIGGO Vision** – Understand and analyse images.
- 🎙️ **BIGGO Voice** – Interact using voice.

## 💎 Plans

- **Free Plan** – Access to basic BIGGO AI features.
- **Premium Plan** – Access to additional features and higher usage limits.

## 🔐 Privacy

BIGGO AI aims to protect users' privacy and keep their conversations private.
